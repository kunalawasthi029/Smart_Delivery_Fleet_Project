E-Commerce Logistics and Delivery Dataset

15,000 synthetic e-commerce orders with 36 features for logistics analytics,
delivery prediction, supply-chain optimization, shipping-cost analysis, and
customer satisfaction modeling.

Primary targets: Delivery_Delay_Hours and On_Time_Delivery_Flag.

Includes order details, regions, shipping methods, delivery partners, package
characteristics, distance, warehouse processing, traffic/weather delays,
courier performance, warehouse capacity, inventory, promotions, delivery
outcomes, shipping/logistics costs, and customer satisfaction.

Collection Methodology:
Fully synthetic Python-generated data using controlled statistical distributions
and business relationships. Delivery performance is influenced by shipping
method, distance, warehouse processing, traffic, weather, capacity, inventory,
courier experience, and service quality.

Provenance: Synthetic / artificially generated. No real customer, company,
address, or personally identifiable information is included.

Update Frequency: Static / not updated.
